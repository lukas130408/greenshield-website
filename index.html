Who We Are

Football expertise meets financial know-how.

Greenshield smoothens volatile cash flows of football clubs and associations through swap deals, ensuring greater financial predictability, which is crucial for all professional organisations when planning future investments.

We combine the needs of the football industry with an understanding of what the financial industry can offer.

Our Products

Two products, one goal.

Swap of potential prize money for risk-adjusted, secured income in domestic, UEFA and FIFA competitions.

  • Hedge on-pitch results before or even during a competition.
  • Clubs and associations keep the income if they do not qualify or perform – Greenshield absorbs the loss.
  • Risks are covered by international insurance companies with a minimum rating of A-.
Learn more about CPS

Swap of conditional bonus clauses in transfer agreements for risk-adjusted, secured income.

  • Selling clubs sell conditional transfer clauses to Greenshield upfront with a risk discount.
  • Clubs keep the income if the transfer conditions are not met – Greenshield absorbs the loss.
  • Greenshield receives the transfer amount if the conditions are met.
