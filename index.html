Our Products

Innovative Financing Solutions for a Stronger Football Future

Two products, one goal: turning uncertain football revenues into risk-adjusted, secured income.

Club Performance Swaps

Swap of potential prize money for risk-adjusted, secured income in domestic, UEFA and FIFA competitions.

  • Allows clubs and football associations to hedge their on-pitch results before the start of a competition or even during the competition.
  • Clubs and FAs retain the income if they do not qualify or perform – Greenshield absorbs the loss.
  • Greenshield receives the prize money if clubs or FAs are successful.
  • The business model is based on cooperation with international insurance companies (minimum rating A-) that cover the risks.
Read Details

Player Performance Swaps

Swap of conditional bonus clauses in transfer agreements for risk-adjusted, secured income.

  • Selling clubs can sell conditional transfer clauses to Greenshield upfront with a risk discount.
  • Clubs retain the income if the transfer conditions are not met – Greenshield absorbs the loss.
  • Greenshield receives the transfer amount if the transfer conditions are met.

What types of financial solutions does Greenshield offer?

Greenshield provides swap-based financing solutions tailored for football clubs and associations. Our products are designed to smooth cash flow, reduce volatility and support sustainable growth.

How does swap-based financing work?

Our swap-based financing involves structured agreements that help clubs manage cash flow fluctuations, providing predictable financial stability that aids long-term planning.

Who can benefit from Greenshield’s services?

Our services are ideal for European football clubs and associations seeking financial stability and support for their investment plans.

What is the process for getting started?

The process begins with a consultation in which we assess your club’s unique financial needs. From there, we design, implement and support a tailored financing solution that aligns with your goals.

Does Greenshield operate internationally?

Yes. While our offices are located in Austria and the Czech Republic, we partner with football clubs across Europe to provide financing solutions.

Is there a minimum club size or revenue requirement?

Our services are adaptable to the needs of clubs of various sizes. We encourage any club interested in financial stability to reach out for a consultation.
