Privacy Policy

Information on how Greenshield ONE GmbH processes personal data.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The controller responsible for processing personal data on this website within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Greenshield ONE GmbH
Radetzkystraße 1, 3100 St. Pölten, Austria
Email: office@greenshield.company

If you have any questions about data protection, you can contact us at any time using the details above.

2. Overview

We keep data processing on this website to a minimum:

  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • Fonts are served from our own website, so no connection to external font services (such as Google Fonts) is made.
  • We only receive personal data if you contact us via the contact form or by email.

3. Hosting and Server Log Files

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, and GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands.

When you visit our website, your browser automatically transmits technical information to the hosting provider’s servers, such as your IP address, the page requested and information about your browser. This is technically necessary to deliver the website to you. According to GitHub, the IP address of every visitor to a GitHub Pages site is logged and stored for security purposes. We ourselves do not have access to these log files and do not use them to identify visitors.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of our website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

As GitHub, Inc. is based in the USA, data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on the basis of which the European Commission has recognised an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR). Further information can be found in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Contact Form

If you send us an enquiry via our contact form, we process the details you provide: your name, email address, message and, if you choose to provide them, your phone number and club or organisation. We use this data exclusively to handle your enquiry and any follow-up questions.

Providing your details is voluntary and not required by law or contract. However, without your name, email address and message we cannot answer your enquiry.

To transmit the form, we use the service Web3Forms (web3forms.com), operated by Web3Creative, India, which acts as our processor (Art. 28 GDPR). Web3Forms receives the content of the form together with your IP address and forwards it to us by email. According to its own information, Web3Forms stores form submissions for a maximum of three years and uses infrastructure providers (including Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner) as well as spam protection services (CleanTalk and Akismet), to which your IP address and email address may be transmitted.

As a result, your data may be processed outside the EU, in particular in India and the USA. Such transfers are based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) included in the Web3Forms Data Processing Agreement. Further information can be found in the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the checkbox in the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and, where your enquiry relates to a possible business relationship, the taking of steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by sending an email to office@greenshield.company.

5. Contact by Email

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the information contained in your message in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, where your enquiry relates to a possible business relationship, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Our email service is provided by Zone Media OÜ, Estonia, which stores and processes emails on our behalf in European data centres.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We delete the data from your enquiry once it has been fully dealt with and it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations (for example under tax or commercial law) require us to keep it for longer. If your enquiry leads to a business relationship, the data is kept for the duration of that relationship and the applicable statutory retention periods.

Copies of contact form submissions stored by Web3Forms are deleted automatically by Web3Forms after three years (see section 4).

7. Links to Other Websites

Our website contains links to external websites, for example to Calendly (appointment booking), LinkedIn and our partners. These websites are not embedded in our pages: no data is sent to these providers when you merely visit our website. Only when you click such a link and open the external website does the respective provider process your data, under its own responsibility and in accordance with its own privacy policy.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • the right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • the right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • the right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • the right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • the right to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

To exercise your rights, simply send an email to office@greenshield.company.

If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.

9. No Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy if our website or the legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
