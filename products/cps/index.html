Club Performance Swaps

Greenshield’s mission is to convert uncertain revenues into secure ones by transforming unpredictable football club earnings, such as potential prize money, into guaranteed income. We achieve this through our innovative product: Club Performance Swaps.

Advantages

Our competitive advantages.

Greenshield has already successfully hedged the sporting risks of clubs in UEFA competitions, empowering them to manage their finances with certainty. Our product lines include the swap of potential prize money for UEFA and FIFA qualifications as well as for UEFA and FIFA competitions. They are also suitable for domestic competitions, such as relegation and promotion battles.

Experienced Leadership

Our team combines senior management experience from professional football clubs with expertise in investment and risk.

Algorithm-Based Model

A proprietary algorithm, developed in-house, that calculates the probability of sporting outcomes – such as qualification, advancement or final league position.

Superior Solution

No upfront payments – a unique solution in the market with high cost efficiency.

Coverage

Competitions we cover.

Club Performance Swaps can be structured for a wide range of national and international competitions.

UEFA Club Qualification Phases

The UEFA club qualification phases determine which teams across Europe advance to prestigious tournaments such as the UEFA Champions League and the UEFA Europa League.

UEFA Club Competitions

UEFA club competitions bring together Europe’s top football clubs in thrilling tournaments, including the UEFA Champions League, Europa League and Conference League.

FIFA & UEFA Association Qualification Phases

FIFA and UEFA association qualification phases determine which national teams qualify for global and European competitions.

FIFA & UEFA Association Competitions

FIFA and UEFA association competitions unite national teams worldwide in a celebration of football excellence.

Domestic Leagues

Domestic leagues are the heartbeat of football in each country, with clubs competing for national glory and top positions that often lead to qualification for UEFA and FIFA tournaments.
