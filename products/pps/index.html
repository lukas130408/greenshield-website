Player Performance Swaps

Greenshield’s mission is to convert uncertain revenues into secure ones by transforming unpredictable football club earnings, such as potential conditional transfer fees, into guaranteed income. Conditional bonus clauses in transfer agreements are huge off-balance-sheet assets that clubs cannot monetise – an undiscovered source of financing. We unlock it through our innovative product: Player Performance Swaps.

Liquidity From Bonus Clauses

Conditional bonus clauses are off-balance-sheet assets. We turn them into secured income today – without further obligations if the conditions are not met.

Key Facts

Greenshield’s game-changing financial solution.

A typical example: a selling club transfers several players to various buying clubs, with additional conditional bonus clauses that may span the next few years. These bonuses depend on specific achievements – for instance, the player reaching a target number of games or goals, or the buying club avoiding relegation or winning the league title.

To navigate these complexities, Greenshield has developed a proprietary in-house algorithm that calculates the probability of both individual player performances and sporting outcomes. It then applies an individual risk discount to the offered swap price, ensuring fair terms for selling clubs.

This allows Greenshield to provide a tailored solution: a portfolio of potential conditional bonus clauses is swapped for risk-adjusted, secured income. The club’s income is guaranteed without further obligations if the conditions are not fulfilled, while the bonus payments go to Greenshield if the conditions are met.
